# Portrait prompts for OpenClaw

One image per person below. Save each one to **`assets/portraits/<file>`** using the exact filename given.
Then commit them to the `Y2JCPA/WhatsDaf` repo on the branch `claude/inspiring-newton-xjnxds`.

## Output spec (same for every image)

- Square **640 × 640**, saved as **JPEG, quality 85** (keep each file under about 150 KB).
- A single person, head and shoulders, roughly centered. The face must still read clearly at 40 px, because
  WhatsApp-style avatars are cropped to a circle and shown small. Keep the face inside the middle 70%.
- Leave out text, letters, watermarks, frames and borders. Hebrew lettering in particular tends to come out garbled.

## Shared style (put this at the front of every prompt)

> Painterly realistic portrait, warm candlelit oil-painting style, soft golden rim light, rich earthy colors,
> shallow depth of field. Head-and-shoulders of a single figure, facing three-quarters toward the viewer,
> calm intelligent expression. Late-antique Near Eastern dress: wool or linen robes, a tallit-style mantle,
> and a wrapped head covering. Background softly blurred. Style matches a set of portraits of Talmudic sages.
> No text, no lettering, no frame.

Then add the line for the person.

**Babylonian Amoraim** (Rav Huna, Rav Pappa, Rav Yosef, Rav Naḥman bar Yitzḥak, Rabba bar bar Ḥana): use a
Babylonian background, either mudbrick academy walls and oil lamps, or palm groves along the Euphrates.
**Tannaim and the Amoraim of the Land of Israel**: use a Galilean or Judean background, either limestone arches and
olive trees, or the Yavne study hall.
